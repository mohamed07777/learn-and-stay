About Amazement Co. Education Division

Amazement Co. is dedicated to bridging the gap between aspiring medical students and world-class educational opportunities. Our Education Division specializes in providing comprehensive, end-to-end support for students from India, Pakistan, the Middle East, and Africa aiming to pursue MBBS degrees in reputable universities in Georgia and Russia.

Our Vision

"Bringing quality medical education closer to students by ensuring safety, affordability, and complete pastoral care."

Accreditation & Partnerships

We partner exclusively with universities that are recognized by major international bodies, including the Medical Council of India (MCI) / National Medical Commission (NMC), World Directory of Medical Schools (WDMS), and local governmental accreditation bodies in Georgia and Russia. This ensures your degree is globally valid.
